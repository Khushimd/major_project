About

Designed to Support Clinical Decisions

Melanoma CDSS is an AI-assisted decision support tool used during an in-person consultation. The doctor enters patient details, uploads a dermoscopic image, and reviews the AI-assisted prediction with the patient present.

Grad-CAM heatmaps appear only when the prediction is melanoma, to show model attention. Rule-based AJCC staging uses clinical and pathology information entered by the doctor. The generated report can later be opened by the patient with the phone number recorded at the visit.
